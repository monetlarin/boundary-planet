Boundary Planet

Requests fly at a little solar system of three planets. Yours is one of them. Move your planet and spin it so the right side meets each request. Something that bounces off your shield flies away, and it might land on a teammate.

No. A shield. Bounces a request away. Where it goes next depends on the angle.
Negotiate. A dock. Catches a request, shrinks it, and lands it later.
Yes. Open ground. Whatever lands is accepted whole.
MoveDrag your planet, or use WASD. SpinDrag anywhere else in the sky, or use the arrow keys (Q and E work too).

Senders: Noise 🔔, Friends ☕ and heavy Boss requests 📅. Everything bounces off a No. Heavy work comes back to someone, so aim it at a teammate who has room. Anything that hits the sun blows up. The whole team scores together.

Solo plays with two computer teammates. A room seats up to three people; empty seats are played by the computer. Tesseros · Prototype v0.7 · One season, about two minutes

How Points and Energy Work

Points are shared by the whole team. Energy is yours alone.

RequestNo (shield)Negotiate (dock)Yes (open ground)
Noise 🔔+1. Bounces away.−1 clutter (1 space for 30 seconds).−1 clutter (1 space for 30 seconds).
Friend ☕−1. Bounces off and asks a teammate next. Leaves only when all three planets have said No.+2 home (1 space, stays).+3 tree (1 space, stays), −0.5 energy. Once it grows: +1.5 energy back.
Boss 📅0. Bounces, but heavy work comes back to someone. Three in a row cracks your shield for 5 seconds.+2 office (2 spaces for 25 seconds), −1 energy.+1 tower (4 spaces for 40 seconds), −3 energy.

Hand-offs. Boss work you bounce that a teammate catches: +2 if they had energy to spare (5 or more). If they were tired: −1, and they lose 1.5 energy.

Room. Your planet has 22 spaces. Trees and homes stay all season; work finishes and frees its space; clutter fades. When you're full, every new Yes crushes your oldest things to make space, and you lose the points they earned (a crushed tree is −3).

The sun. Its gravity pulls on anything you bounce, and anything that hits it blows up. Your aim line curves to show where a bounce will go, with a ring if it will reach the sun. A friend lost in the sun costs −1.

Energy. You start with 10 of 12. It refills slowly, about 1 every 25 seconds, and grown trees give some back. Low energy makes you slower to move and turn. At zero: playing solo, your season ends; in a room, you rest for 6 seconds with your shield and dock down, and the team loses 3.

Tiers. Sprouting at 25, Growing at 60, Flourishing at 110, Thriving at 170.
